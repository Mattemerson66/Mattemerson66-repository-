    Daily reminder

    A home-screen web app can't reliably send its own notifications on iPhone, so this adds a repeating event with an alert to your Calendar instead.

    Add daily reminder to Calendar

    Opens an "Add to Calendar" screen. Tap Add All. It starts from the next time you picked and stays at that time through daylight saving changes. To change the time, delete the old event in Calendar and add a new one.

    Nothing happens when you tap it? , then open it from the Files app.

    Prefer a Shortcuts automation?
    1. Open the Shortcuts app, then tap Automation, then +.
    2. Choose Time of Day, pick your time, set it to Daily and Run Immediately.
    3. Add the action Open App and pick Good, the icon you added to your Home Screen.

    That opens the journal automatically each day. The Calendar reminder is gentler because it only nudges you.

    Backup

    Entries are stored only on this device and are never uploaded. Export a backup and save it to Files or iCloud Drive now and then.

    Why this works

    Health, family, a roof over your head. Those answers aren't wrong, they're just too big to feel anything. Real gratitude lives somewhere smaller: a moment someone made you laugh, a task that went smoother than you feared, a parking spot right away.

    Your brain already spends most of its time scanning for problems. Specific gratitude turns that lens around.
